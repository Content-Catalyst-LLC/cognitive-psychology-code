# Measurement Notes

## Organizational cognitive burden index

A simple index can be constructed as:

L_org = information_load + uncertainty_level + coordination_load + institutional_pressure

A more advanced version can include interaction terms:

L_org = w_i I + w_u U + w_c C + w_p P + w_f F - w_s S

where:

- I = information load
- U = uncertainty load
- C = coordination load
- P = institutional pressure
- F = feedback delay
- S = psychological safety

The subtraction of psychological safety reflects the possibility that safer climates reduce effective cognitive burden by allowing correction, dissent, and early error detection.

## Recommended reliability checks

- Internal consistency of multi-item scales
- Inter-rater agreement for decision-quality ratings
- Test-retest reliability for repeated decision tasks
- Sensitivity analysis for burden-index weights
- Model comparison across linear, logistic, hierarchical, and nonlinear specifications

## Recommended validity checks

- Convergent validity with workload or NASA-TLX-style measures
- Predictive validity against later decision outcomes
- Discriminant validity against general intelligence or experience
- Ecological validity in field or scenario-based organizational tasks
