# Research Protocol

This protocol supports empirical and simulated research on skill acquisition and expertise development.

## Constructs

- **Practice exposure:** cumulative session count, hours, and task repetitions.
- **Deliberate practice:** structured, effortful, goal-directed practice focused on specific weaknesses.
- **Feedback quality:** timely, specific, interpretable, and actionable performance information.
- **Learning curve:** improvement in accuracy, speed, error reduction, or stability over time.
- **Automaticity:** reduced need for active working-memory control while preserving accuracy.
- **Chunking:** organization of separate elements into larger meaningful units.
- **Pattern recognition:** rapid detection of meaningful domain-specific configurations.
- **Transfer:** application of trained skill to new tasks or contexts.
- **Adaptive expertise:** flexible use and revision of skill under novelty or uncertainty.

## Recommended designs

1. Longitudinal learning-curve study.
2. Deliberate-practice intervention.
3. Feedback-quality manipulation.
4. Novice-expert comparison.
5. Transfer and retention assessment.
6. Time-pressure or cognitive-load manipulation.
7. Human-AI coaching or decision-support study.

## Cautions

Do not equate repetition with deliberate practice. Do not treat faster response time as expertise unless accuracy, retention, and transfer remain strong. Model participant and task effects. Separate routine automaticity from adaptive expertise.
